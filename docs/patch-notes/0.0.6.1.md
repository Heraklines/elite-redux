# PokeRogue Redux v0.0.6.1

This update adds several new ways to play, expands postgame progression, and fixes a large batch of battle, save, sprite, and multi-battle issues reported by players.

## New ways to play

- **Fun Mode:** build a 200-wave run from configurable Pokemon, type, ability, learnset, stat, evolution, item, weather, move, Ability Avalanche, Moody, and Mega randomizers.
- **Moody Mode:** draft persistent boons and upgrades while the run adds curses and enemy boons every ten waves.
- **Classic Sprint:** play a complete Classic or Challenge run in 100 waves with compressed progression, encounters, shops, and rewards.
- **Postgame Endless:** continue a completed supported run with its party and build intact, then face Rifts, player Ghost teams, recurring rivals, raids, and a new deep-Endless finale.

Random Pokemon caught in Fun Mode can join the current team and still award candy, but they do not unlock species, forms, abilities, natures, or shiny tiers in starter select. When Item Chaos is active, the same restriction applies to shiny catches.

## Progression and rewards

- Added depth- and difficulty-scaled Shiny, Candy, and Voucher multipliers with a breakdown panel in post-battle shops.
- Added the Move Randomizer and Greater Move Randomizer items.
- Replaced Ability Charm with Mystery Event Charm, which doubles Mystery Event encounter odds.
- DNA Splicers now correctly occupy Ultra Ball tier.
- Added player leaderboards and profiles to ER Stats.
- Eternatus can now hatch from the regular Legendary Egg pool, while remaining excluded from the rotating Legendary focus pool.

## Ghosts and Endless

- Late Hell trainers can be replaced by victorious player Ghost teams.
- Ghosts now retain saved forms, randomized abilities and innates, held items, relics, and black-shiny presentation.
- Endless Rifts pulse every five Endless waves, or every three waves with Sprint pacing.
- Endless raids occur every 50 equivalent waves and now use doubled wild-boss shield segments.
- Expanded Ghost variety and improved long-run enemy, ability, relic, and raid scaling.
- Added the Rift Ledger to the pause menu.

## Balance

- Ordinary damaging moves begin at 40% power on wave 1 and scale to full power by wave 30, or wave 15 in Sprint.
- Psychic Terrain and priority-blocking abilities now stop priority spread moves, including priority granted by abilities, boons, and other temporary effects.
- Telekinetic now affects only the first living opposing lead instead of every opposing Pokemon in doubles and triples.

## Major fixes

- Reduced enemy-command and ability-processing stalls in doubles and triples.
- Fixed Triple Battle reloads, fainted-slot reconstruction, incorrect command labels, carried-over formations, and invisible Pokemon after switching.
- Restored the full Shattered Psyche/Primal Mew fusion and defusion lifecycle.
- Fixed Gallantry failing on the first attempt of a battle.
- Fixed Disguise immediately restoring itself in fog.
- Corrected numerous move flags and interactions, including Rip and Tear, Spectral Serenade, Shell Side Arm, Fury Swipes, Skull Bash, Rock Climb, Shocking Jab, Aerilate, and Multi Lens.
- Fixed black shinies appearing as red shinies or losing their tier, palette, GIFT data, or saved-team identity.
- Improved regional split-evolution labels so similar form names can be distinguished.
- Fixed same-species eggs changing result after reload.
- Fixed cloud loading choosing an older device copy over a newer cloud save.
- Fixed malformed held-item data, unavailable fusion sprites, missing animation frames, and disappearing targets causing crashes or stalls.
- Added a dedicated mobile control for the upgraded Map and made the pause menu scrollable.

## Still gated

Co-op and Tournament Mode remain unavailable in production. Showdown also remains unavailable with its existing notice while its release work continues.
