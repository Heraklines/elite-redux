## 10. Contract and harness gaps (for the foundation owner and B20, not library files)

- **Attr-level conditions have no declaration slot.** `AbAttr.addCondition` / `conditionalAttr` conditions (not constructor arguments)
  cannot be written in `DeclCondition` (slots are only `ctor:N` and `gate`). Users in this chunk: Evaporate's
  UserFieldSelfStatDropImmunityAbAttr (`getTagOnSide(MIST, own side)`, maps exactly to `field.arena-tag{ MIST, holder-side }`) and
  Shields Down's StatusEffectImmunityAbAttr (`p.formIndex < 7`, needs `mon.form-index{holder, lt, 7}` from chunk 1). The rebuild
  copies both unchanged, so behaviour is identical, but staff cannot see or change them in the block. Suggested: a slot `attr` whose
  closure is `AbAttrCondition` (`ctx: "holder"`), bound with `bindCondition(abilityId, attrIndex, spec, "attr")`.
- **L2 cannot reach three classes' triggers**: (1) a holder that lowers ITS OWN stat (SelfStatDropImmunityAbAttr,
  UserFieldSelfStatDropImmunityAbAttr), template wanted: holder uses Superpower (Close Combat, Overheat) against a Snorlax;

  `startingWave(2)` so the battle really is a double battle; (3) the holder inflicts a status (ConfusionOnStatusEffectAbAttr),
  template wanted: holder uses Toxic (never missing) on a Snorlax. Until those templates exist the shared L2 run reports
  "never evaluated its attr" for those attrs, and `b04-chunk2.test.ts` proves the same twins by hand (real battles, original ability
  against its rebuilt shadow, plus a direct drive of every input).
- **L1 has no engine-parameter builder for the cancel triggers** (`cancel.stat-*`, `cancel.status-*`) nor for `event.leave-field`
  and `event.after-inflicting-status`, so the L1 attr differential skips them (`noParamBuilder`). `b04-chunk2.test.ts` drives
