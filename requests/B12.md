## For the librarian (B19)

### R-B12-01  Atom `ko.credited-to-me`  (StatTriggerOnKoAbAttr, nine users are legacy-closure until it exists)

- Label: "I am the Pokemon credited with the knock out". Group "Party" (or "Time and memory"). `requires: ["other"]` (the fainted Pokemon,
  slot `other` of "When I knock out a Pokemon"). No params. `pure: true`.
- Formula: `other.turnData?.attacksReceived?.[0]?.sourceId === holder.id`.
  Source: `src/data/elite-redux/archetypes/stat-trigger-on-event.ts`, `StatTriggerOnKoAbAttr.gate` clause 3 (the Moxie semantics: the victim's
  most recent attacker is the holder). The library trigger carries the same test as part of its moment gate
  (`compile-composition.ts` MOMENT_GATES `event.on-ko`), so the atom just exposes it.
- Same predicate as B09's R-B09-32 (guard `credited-with-the-knock-out`); the librarian may keep one of the two names. As an atom it is
  better: Forsaken Heart (any faint) is the same rule WITHOUT it, which is a design choice staff may flip.
- Binding of the nine users once it exists: `all[ not side.is{self}, ko.credited-to-me ]`. Users (all legacy-closure today, slot `gate`):
  ChillngNeigh (5003 attr 0), Adrenaline Rush (5185 attr 0), Super Strain (5217 attr 0), Hubris (5262 attr 0), Haunting Frenzy (5301 attr 1),
  Dragon's Ritual (5400 attr 0), Breezy Neigh (5559 attr 0), Hydra (5630 attr 1), As One (20022 attr 0).
- Also correct the label of WHEN `event.on-ko` ("When I knock out a Pokemon"): `PostKnockOutAbAttr` is dispatched for EVERY Pokemon on the field
  once ANY Pokemon faints (`faint-phase.ts:268`), it is not "I knock out". B03 asked for the same fix.
