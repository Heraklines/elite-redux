### Engine-query sweep

`SpiritualSaberNoContactAbAttr` and `ManifestContactAbAttr` are read by class name in `Move.doesFlagEffectApply` (MAKES_CONTACT). The
"only slicing moves" rule of Spiritual Saber lives in `move.ts`, not in the class, so its property cannot be a setting. If the
`abilityQuery` sweep routes "does this move make contact?" through the ability, please move that rule into the class (an
`appliesTo(move)` method or a property argument) so staff can change it.

### Harness

`test/tests/elite-redux/decomp/harness/world.ts` (`PARAM_BUILDERS`) got two entries, because L1 refuses a Tier B class without an
engine-parameter builder for its trigger: `event.faint` (Slab's Curse) and `event.item-lost` (Booby Trap). B13 added another entry
(`calc.trap-check`) in the same place, so a merge conflict there is resolved by keeping every entry. The L2 scenario
`foe-removes-item` never reaches `event.item-lost` (Knock Off hurts the holder but leaves a starting berry in place), see the notes
of `BoobyTrapItemLostAbAttr.decl.ts`; its probe now steals the item with Thief, which does reach the moment. The scenario owner (B20) may
want to look at the Knock Off case.
