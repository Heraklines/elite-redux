### R-B15-1 add `RapierFlagInjectionAbAttr` and `ChivalryAbAttr` to the `flagKeys` of WHEN `while.active`

`vocabulary/when-registry.ts`, WHEN `while.active`, `flagKeys`. Both classes are never dispatched (a presence marker the engine finds by constructor name:
`Move.doesFlagEffectApply` for Rapier, `pokemonCarriesChivalry` in `Pokemon.damageAndUpdate` and `SwitchSummonPhase` for Chivalry). Their declarations use
`when: "while.active"`; L0 reports `when "while.active" dispatches ..., none of which is the class or one of its ancestors` until the names are in that list.
Same one-line change as R-B09-11. Used by Rapier (6079) and Chivalry (5909). Chunk 2 adds a third name: `CommonRootAbAttr` (Common Root, 5904), a presence marker read by `SeedTag.lapse` in `src/data/battler-tags.ts` by constructor name.

### R-B15-2 moment "When my ally takes a direct hit" and effect "Take a share of an ally's damage" (group Being hit / Damage), for Chivalry

Today the doubles half of Chivalry lives in `Pokemon.damageAndUpdate` (src/field/pokemon.ts, the call `damage -= erApplyChivalry(this, damage)`), for DIRECT damage with a source
and damage > 0. Needed:
- WHEN `event.ally-takes-direct-hit`, provides `holder` (the Chivalry holder), `ally` (the victim), `damage`, `other` (the attacker). A frame builder that reads the first living, active ally of the victim that carries the attr.
- DO `effect.take-share-of-ally-damage { share }`: the holder takes `toDmgValue(damage * share, 1)` as RAW HP loss (`damageAndUpdate(share, { result: INDIRECT })` on the field, a plain `hp = max(0, hp - share)` write off the field, never a faint phase off the field) and the same amount is removed from the victim's damage.
- IF atoms that exist today are enough for the rest (`party.doubles`, `side`).
Used by Chivalry (5909).

### R-B15-3 moment "When I switch out by choice" with a "marked replacement" effect (group Entry and exit), for Chivalry

The singles half marks the incoming Pokemon in `SwitchSummonPhase` (src/phases/switch-summon-phase.ts, the `markChivalryRedirect` call) when the holder leaves by the menu "Switch"
command (`switchType === SWITCH` and `turnCommands[...].command === POKEMON`). Needed:
- WHEN `event.switch-out-by-choice` (a voluntary switch, not a forced one), provides `holder`, `ally` (the replacement).
- DO `effect.redirect-damage-to-me { share, turns }`: the replacement carries a mark `{ holder, expiryTurn = battle turn + turns, share }`; while the mark lasts, each DIRECT hit on it moves `toDmgValue(damage * share, 1)` to the holder as raw HP loss (the holder may be off the field and then only loses HP).
Used by Chivalry (5909).

### R-B15-4 effect "Give foes extra types from their own team" (group Types and immunities), for Bad Splice

`BadSpliceAbAttr` is declared as a package because the vocabulary has no effect that adds types to a Pokemon and no memory of what was added. If the librarian adds
`effect.graft-types { sourceMemberCount }` (types of N random other living teammates of each foe, only the ones the foe lacks, undone when the holder leaves) the package can be split into
a WHEN plus this effect. Used by Bad Splice (5932). Low priority: single use.

### R-B15-5 effect "Swap a raw stat with the fastest foe, fading back over N turns" (group Stats), for Borrowed Time

`BorrowedTimeSummonAbAttr` and `BorrowedTimeDecayAbAttr` are packages. A library effect `effect.swap-stat-with-foe { stat, turns }` (stores the originals, restores them exactly after `turns` turn ends) would replace both
with one WHEN plus one effect. Used by Borrowed Time (5910). Low priority: single use.

## Chunk 2 of 6 (Cleansing Light, Common Root, Crosscut x2, Dandelion Burst, Draconic Voodoo, Genesis Supernova, Heartbreak)

### R-B15-6 condition "The move has the {flag} property as printed" (group Move), for Crosscut (both attrs) and Draconic Voodoo

Formula: `move.hasFlag(MoveFlags[flag])` on the move's own printed flags, NOT `Move.doesFlagEffectApply`. The existing `move.flag` calls `doesFlagEffectApply` (src/data/moves/move.ts:994),
which also answers true for flags that an ability injects (Rapier, Taekkyeon and the other flag injectors) and strips contact for several abilities. Crosscut (src/data/elite-redux/abilities/crosscut.ts,
`isCrosscutTypedMove`: `move.category !== STATUS && (hasFlag(SLICING_MOVE) || hasFlag(PULSE_MOVE))`) and the Draconic Voodoo hit seam (`move.hasFlag(BITING_MOVE)`) use the printed flag, so the existing atom is a different predicate.
Requires `move`. With it, the two Crosscut gates become `all[ move.damaging, any[ printed SLICING, printed PULSE ] ]` (plus `not move.multi-hit` for the second strike); today both gates are `bindLegacyClosure`.
Used by Crosscut second strike (5908 attr 0), Crosscut power rider (5908 attr 1) and Draconic Voodoo (5930).

### R-B15-7 scope for `time.once-per` that shares a memory the class owns (group Time), for Dandelion Burst

Dandelion Burst (src/data/elite-redux/abilities/dandelion-burst.ts) remembers "used this wave" in its own module WeakMap (holder -> wave index): `DANDELION_BURST_USED.get(pokemon) === (currentBattle?.waveIndex ?? 0)` blocks the trigger, and
`erDandelionBurstUsedWave` hands the same value to the AI snapshot (ai/combat-private-state.ts, "ability-state:dandelion-burst"). `time.once-per` scope `wave` reads `waveData.entryEffectsFired`, a different memory, and writing a key
there would change the AI state (`combat-engine-adapter.ts` serialises that Set). Needed: either a scope `wave-index` that reads and writes a caller-supplied `WeakMap<Pokemon, number>` keyed by the current wave index, or the librarian's decision to
move the class to the shared Set and to update the AI reader to match. Today the check lives in the class `guards()` and is listed as feasibility. Used by Dandelion Burst (5907).

### R-B15-8 effect "Plant Leech Seed on every foe" = implement `effect.apply-tag` for the opposing side (group Status conditions), for Dandelion Burst

`effect.apply-tag` exists but is `implemented: false`. Dandelion Burst applies, per active foe in field order, `opponent.addTag(BattlerTagType.SEEDED, undefined, MoveId.LEECH_SEED, holder.id)` (SeedTag.canAdd refuses Grass types) and then
`unshiftNew("StatStageChangePhase", foeIndex, false, [stat], stages)`. To split the class into WHEN + gate + two effects the tag effect needs a target "all foes", a `sourceMove` param (the tag needs its source move and source id) and the same
per-foe order as the stat phase. `effect.stat-stage` on the opposing side exists. Used by Dandelion Burst (5907). Low priority: single use.

### R-B15-9 target "my weakest living ally" for `effect.heal-percent` and `effect.cure-status` (group Healing), for Cleansing Light

`CleansingLightAbAttr.lowestAlly`: `pokemon.getAllies().filter(a => a?.isActive(true) && !a.isFainted())`, then the one with the lowest `hp` (the first of equals). Heal: `toDmgValue(maxHp * fraction)` through `PokemonHealPhase`, skipped at full HP;
cure: `ally.resetStatus()` plus a message. Used by Cleansing Light (5912).

### R-B15-10 condition "I scored {cmp} {n} KOs this turn" (group Time), for Cleansing Light

State: a per-holder record `{ key: wave:turn, count }` bumped once per KO and restarted when the turn changes (`CLEANSING_LIGHT_KO_COUNT`, read by the AI snapshot through `erCleansingLightKoState`). The count includes the current KO.
`time.nth-use` counts per battle and is a different memory. With R-B15-9 and this atom the class becomes WHEN `event.on-win` + heal-percent + (IF KOs this turn >= 2) cure-status. Used by Cleansing Light (5912).

### R-B15-11 moment "When a foe loses HP to Leech Seed" and effect "Heal every ally by the drained amount" (group Healing), for Common Root

`SeedTag.lapse` (src/data/battler-tags.ts, the block "ER Common Root") heals EVERY active ally of the seeder by the same drained amount when any Pokemon on the seeder's side carries the marker, unless Liquid Ooze reversed the drain.
Needed: WHEN `event.leech-seed-drain` providing `holder`, `other` (the seeded foe), `damage` (the drained amount), and DO `effect.heal-allies-by-amount` (each active ally not at full HP, `PokemonHealPhase(ally, amount, "seededLapse")`).
The class is a presence marker today (engine-query). Used by Common Root (5904).

### R-B15-12 moment "When my attack damages a target" (group Attacking), for Draconic Voodoo

`erBatch4OnTargetHit` (src/data/elite-redux/abilities/batch4-on-hit.ts), called from `MoveEffectPhase.applyOnTargetEffects` once per target per hit of an attacking move AFTER the Batch-3 seam, with a `damaging` flag.
It is not the same moment as `event.after-my-hit` (PostAttackAbAttr). Needed: WHEN `event.my-attack-damages-target` providing `holder`, `other` (the target), `move`, `moveUser`; with IF `target is still standing` (`!target.isFainted()`),
`move.type` and R-B15-6. Together with the graft effect of R-B15-4 (`effect.graft-types`, here with a fixed type instead of the team's types) the on-hit half of Draconic Voodoo becomes WHEN + IF + DO. Used by Draconic Voodoo (5930).

### R-B15-13 effect "Link to my nearest ally" and moment "When my linked ally faints" (group Entry and exit), for Heartbreak

`formLink` (src/data/elite-redux/abilities/link.ts) links the holder to its nearest adjacent living ally (one seeded draw only when several qualify, triples), stored in a module WeakMap and torn down lazily when either end leaves.
`erHeartbreakOnAllyFaint` (called from `FaintPhase.doFaint`) reads the link RAW, breaks it and gives the holder +N Speed and +N in its higher raw attacking stat, then -M Defense and Sp. Def, through `StatStageChangePhase`.
Needed: DO `effect.link-to-ally` and WHEN `event.linked-ally-faints` (provides `holder`, `ally`). Then the faint half is WHEN + effect.stat-stage x2 with a `higher-attack` pick, as Berserk's `statPick`. Used by Heartbreak (5920).
