### R-B18-31  `moveUser` on `calc.move-immunity` and an atom for the raw move property  (MoveFlagImmunityAbAttr, Ball Fetch)

- Why it is a request and not a block today: pokemon.ts:4257 ALSO reads the class by name and calls `blocks(move)`, so a replaceable gate would

### R-B18-36  split `PostAttackStealHeldItemAbAttr.canApply` into a gate and a residual  (ChancePostAttackStealHeldItemAbAttr; owner of ab-attrs.ts)
