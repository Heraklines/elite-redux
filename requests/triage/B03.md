## Disagreements with LIBRARY-DECISIONS.md

Numbers D1 to D10 are shared with `requests/triage/B04.md` (D2, D3, D5 and D8 are B04 items).

- **D1. `event.on-ko` summary went the wrong way.** Section 7 (line 196-197) says the `event.on-ko` summary was
  corrected to drop "any Pokemon faints". The engine says the opposite: `faint-phase.ts:268-270` calls
  `applyAbAttrs("PostKnockOutAbAttr", { pokemon: p, victim })` for every `p` in `inSpeedOrder(BOTH)`, whoever caused
  the faint; the base class doc says "applies after a Pokemon (other than the user) is knocked out" (A:3042); the one B03 user that
  reads it (`CopyFaintedAllyAbilityAbAttr.gate`, A:3085-3087) tests that the victim is on MY side, i.e. an ally fainted,
  and Soul Heart (I:1671) is the "any Pokemon faints" ability. The live text, "Runs after this Pokemon knocks out a
  Pokemon. Example: Moxie raises Attack" (texts-whens.ts:133), and the label "When I knock out a Pokemon"
  (when-registry.ts:198) are both wrong; Moxie is `PostVictoryStatStageChangeAbAttr` (I:1129), i.e. `event.on-win`. A
  composition on `event.on-ko` gets no "credited" check unless it adds the guard `credited-with-the-knock-out`
  (the V1 runner adds it inline, rule-ab-attrs.ts:195-198, which is probably where the wording came from). Fix: label
  "When any Pokemon faints", summary naming Soul Heart / Receiver, drop the Moxie example and synonym.
- **D4. The binding-facility asks are Deferred, not Rejected.** Section 5 (line 142) lists the `extra` / `attr` slots,
  number-valued form closures and new closure contexts under "Rejected". Each is needed and the blocking change is
  named (define-decl.ts:47 `ConditionContext`; build-attr.ts:132-139 only `ctor:N` and `gate`; build-attr.ts:258 copies
  `extraCondition` verbatim). The form-function slot alone blocks the legacy-closure parts of 13 abilities in COV
  (Zen Mode, Shields Down, Schooling, Disguise, Battle Bond, Power Construct, Ice Face, Hunger Switch, Zero to Hero, Tera
  Shift, Patchwork, Two-Faced, Revelation). By the file's own status words (line 21-22) these are Deferred.
- **D6. `effect.ability-form-change` and `effect.trigger-ability-form-change` are one effect under two ids.** Both are
  planned, both are "change to the form the species lists for this ability" (texts-effects.ts:358-361 and 366-369), and
  every class behind them makes the same call `triggerPokemonFormChange(pokemon, SpeciesFormChangeAbilityTrigger[,
  false])` (A:299, A:3789, A:4176, A:5695; `delayed` defaults to false, battle-scene.ts:5039). The naming pass (section
  6) says near-duplicates were resolved; this pair was not. One should become an alias of the other.
- **D7. `PostSummonRemoveEffectAbAttr` is not "an engine clean-up call staff do not reason about".** The
  `UNEXPOSED_DISPATCH_KEYS` reason (dispatch-keys.ts:23-24; predates B19 but B19 left B03-c3's WHEN request
  undecided) misses that every `PostSummonHealStatusAbAttr` and `PostSummonRemoveBattlerTagAbAttr` user fires there
  (both extend the class), so Limber cures paralysis right after a move (move-end-phase.ts:46-51). The declarations of
  those two classes show only the entry trigger.
- **D9. `effect.reset-stat-stages` is listed as answering B03 (section 4, line 116) but cannot express either B03
  class.** Its only setting is `target` with holder / other / holder-side / opposing-side (effects-library.ts:379-388,
  enums.ts:83). `PostSummonClearOpponentPositiveStatStagesAbAttr` (Petrify) resets only RAISED stages of every active
  foe; `PostSummonClearAllyStatStagesAbAttr` resets every active ally with one message each. Missing: `which: all | only
  raised`, the message flag, and the ally / foe targets.
- **D10. `effect.stat-stages` is listed as answering B03 (section 4, line 112) only for the target.** The Intimidate
  rules (c3-46b) and the after-effect (c3-46c) are not answered anywhere; 23 `PostSummonStatStageChangeAbAttr` attrs
  depend on them.
- Agreed and re-checked: `move.has-flag-intrinsic` = `move.inherent-flag`, `mon.is-trapped` = `tag.trapped`,
  `field.terrain-unhidden` for Quark Drive (`getTerrainCondition`, I:2332-2336), the three guards, `rng.chance{alwaysRoll}`,
  `status.has-any`, `tag.has-any`, `status.just-set`, `mon.level`, `mon.form-number`, `party.ally-active`,
  `party.multi-battle`, `hp` `rounded-ratio`, `field.weather-fog`, and the `effect.apply-tag` / `effect.arena-tag`
  deferral.
