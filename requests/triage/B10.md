   `hit.dealt-damage` as `damage > 0` (`conditions-library.ts:25-37`). For R-B10-c3-01 the correct mapping is `hit.landed-damage` /
   guard `hit-connected`; the §5 alias line must drop `hit.dealt-damage`.

   "skips a Pokemon that blocks indirect damage" feasibility that R-B10-08 requires (`effects-library.ts:350-358`).

   and were not credited.
