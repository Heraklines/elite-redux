## Disagreements with LIBRARY-DECISIONS.md

1. LD §7 rewrote the `event.on-ko` summary to "Runs after this Pokemon knocks out a Pokemon" and removed "any Pokemon faints"
   (`texts-whens.ts:132-133`; label "When I knock out a Pokemon", `when-registry.ts:198`). The engine fires it for every
   Pokemon on the field whenever any Pokemon faints: `for (const p of inSpeedOrder(BOTH)) applyAbAttrs("PostKnockOutAbAttr", { pokemon: p, victim })`
   (`src/phases/faint-phase.ts:268-270`), and the base class doc says "after a Pokemon (other than the user) is knocked out,
   including indirectly", `canApply` true (`ab-attrs.ts:3041-3049`). R-B11-C1-12 is right; LD's fix made it worse.
   Relabel to "When a Pokemon on the field faints" (victim in `other`).
2. LD §4 says IgnoreMoveEffects uses `effect.multiply-effect-chance` with multiplier 0. Not equal: the effect refuses Order Up
   and Electro Shot (`evaluate/effects.ts:140-142`); `IgnoreMoveEffectsAbAttr` / `UserFieldIgnoreMoveEffectsAbAttr` zero any
   chance > 0 (`ab-attrs.ts:1804-1809`, `:1822-1823`). Shield Dust style users and B11-07 need a no-exceptions form.
3. LD §1/§5 do not address R-B11-14 / C4-13: `mon.has-ability` still resolves only vanilla `AbilityId`
   (`evaluate/conditions.ts:539`); LD §7 says "move and ability settings accept player names" but ER ability names still throw.
4. LD §8 lists B11 as heading-classified; C2-08 and C2-13 are implemented in the registry and not credited.
